# Changelog / 変更履歴

## Unreleased
- (EN) port_speed (core §3.5 items 3 and 7), as oep-client-python 748c276, after a V003 jig (classic ESP32 + CH340) passed 921600 each way alone but broke a frame every ~0.5 s under sustained duplex use, the probe reverted by itself and the session's lease lapsed: `raiseSpeed` adds a duplex phase after the in / out phases - link_source and link_sink (max_frame) interleaved, at the in-flight that passed, for `duplexSeconds` (1) and at least `duplexFrames` (64); a broken frame fails the rate ("broke both ways at once"). `SpeedTrial.duplexBytes` / `duplexKBs` / `brokenDuplex`, `SpeedReport.duplexKBs`, a duplex column in `speedText`. In use: while a raised rate is in force, the link counts broken frames and resends; 3 within 5 s (`STRIKE_MAX`, `STRIKE_WINDOW_MS`) step down - port_speed step 2 at the raised rate (200 ms, never sent again), the boot speed, a confirm, the request again there; other requests wait for it. A request at a raised rate that gets no answer at all keeps the old fallback, but each wait is now at most a quarter of the lease (at least 300 ms; `Link.waitMs`, the lease bound by Host), so it ends well inside the lease. Either way the rate is not used again in that session (`SpeedReport.steppedDown`, `.downWhy`, a line in `speedText`; `raiseSpeed` skips it). A broken frame twice at a raised rate now falls back too (it used to reach the caller).

- (EN) A serial link whose port a session holds sends a request again as soon as a broken frame (COBS or CRC fails) arrives, instead of after the timeout: the probe sends no raw bytes on a held port (oep-core §3.4), so the broken frame was the reply; the oldest request waiting goes once more with the same corr and the probe answers it from its retry table (one resend; one already resent, or sent with resend off, fails at once with `cobs.CorruptFrame`). Without a session the candidate is still the port's raw bytes, counted as noise and skipped. `Link.held` (bound by `Host` to its session), `Link.resendNow()`; `confirmRaw` reads a broken answer as no answer. As oep-client-python be4b451 (an M5Stack ATOM's 60 KB capture read: 4.0 -> 8.3 KB/s at 115200).
- (JA) セッションが口を持っているシリアルの経路では、壊れたフレーム（COBS が解けない、CRC が合わない）が来たら時間切れを待たずにすぐ送り直す: 持たれている口には probe が生のバイトを送らない（oep-core §3.4）ので、壊れたフレームは応答。待っている一番古い要求を同じ corr でもう一度送り、probe は送り直しの表から答える（送り直しは 1 回。もう送り直した要求と resend を切って送った要求は、すぐ `cobs.CorruptFrame` で失敗する）。セッションが無ければ、候補はこれまでどおり口の生のバイトで、noise に数えて飛ばす。`Link.held`（`Host` が自分のセッションに結ぶ）、`Link.resendNow()`。`confirmRaw` は壊れた応答を応答なしとして読む。oep-client-python be4b451 と同じ（M5Stack ATOM で 60 KB のキャプチャの読み出しが 115200 で 4.0 → 8.3 KB/s）。
- (EN) `raiseSpeed` (oep-core §3.5 at oep-spec d46cc0d, as oep-client-python 1e2b8e4, used on an M5Stack ATOM): after a baud change the link waits `SWITCH_SETTLE_MS` (20 ms) before the first byte, and the new rate is found with a confirm (up to 3, 200 ms each) before anything is measured; a rate whose confirm never comes back says "no confirm at the new rate". The verify runs pipelined at the probe's max_inflight first and, when frames break there (a line that loses bytes while both ways are busy: the ATOM's FTDI at 500 kbaud and up), reads past the leftovers with a confirm and verifies once more one request at a time; the count that passed is `SpeedTrial.inflight` and stays on the link as `Link.inflightCap`, which `Link.exchange` (the host's pipeline) honours until the link is back at the boot speed (end, revert, `backToBase`). `speedText` shows "committed (in flight N)".
- (JA) `raiseSpeed`（oep-spec d46cc0d の oep-core §3.5、oep-client-python 1e2b8e4 と同じ。M5Stack ATOM で使った）: 速さを変えたら最初のバイトの前に `SWITCH_SETTLE_MS`（20 ms）待ち、測る前に confirm（最大 3 回、各 200 ms）で新しい速さを確かめる。confirm が返らない速さは「no confirm at the new rate」と書く。確かめはまず probe の max_inflight でパイプラインで行い、そこでフレームが壊れたら（両方向が同時に動くとバイトを落とす線: ATOM の FTDI の 500 kbaud 以上）残りを confirm で読み飛ばして 1 つずつでもう一度確かめる。通った数は `SpeedTrial.inflight` で、`Link.inflightCap` として link に残り、`Link.exchange`（host のパイプライン）は起動時の速さに戻るまで（end、戻す、`backToBase`）それを守る。`speedText` は「committed (in flight N)」と出す。
