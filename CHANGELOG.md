- (EN) port_speed follows the host guide's reference procedure (oep-spec host-development-guide.ja.md §7; core §3.5 is the handshake), as oep-client-python b0ffdfc. `raiseSpeed(host, candidates = [500000], { flows = null, verify = null, baseline = null, frames = 16, verifyMs, idleMs = 3000, port, record = false })` replaces the old `rates, { verifyBytes, verifySeconds, duplexSeconds, duplexFrames }`. The minimal form (§7.2, the default; ~50 ms, no measurement): try -> switch to the requested baud -> settle 20 ms -> confirm (100 ms x3) -> commit. The full form (`verify: true`, or `flows` given; §7.3): a baseline at the boot speed per flow (this session's frames when 60+ and under 10 %, else 60 measured per flow, re-measured at n=1 when over 10 %), then per candidate every flow (`'in' | 'out' | 'duplex'` or `[flow, n]`; default all three at the most the link keeps) for `frames` (16) frames at max_frame-16, a flow failing on broken+lost >= 3 and a ratio over max(2x baseline, 5 %), re-run at n=1 (then n=1 is the link's cap), one failed flow failing the candidate. `SpeedTrial` now carries `actual`, `switched`, `flows` (`FlowResult`: flow, n, frames, broken, lost, kbS, ratio, passed, gone, name), `nCap`, `inKBs`/`outKBs`/`duplexKBs`; `SpeedReport` adds `verified`, `baseline`, `baselineFrames`, `baselineFlows`, `stepDowns` (`StepDown`: at, rate, why, ratio), `skipped`; `speedText` prints a line per flow. In use the link counts its frames (`Link.count`: good / broken / lost; `baseCounts` at the boot speed, `window` raised) and judges the last 3 s (none under 50 frames) over max(2x baseline, 10 %) broken or lost (`IN_USE_WINDOW_MS`, `IN_USE_MIN_FRAMES`, `IN_USE_FLOOR`; was 3 strikes in 5 s, `STRIKE_MAX` / `STRIKE_WINDOW_MS` / `Link.strikes` are gone); a missed answer still falls back to the boot speed, confirmed within port_speed_idle_max_ms + 1 s (`backToBase` defaults to `OPEN_RETRY_MS`; an Error when no confirm comes, never back to the raised rate). The keepalive interval (`Link.keepaliveMs`) is set under half of idle_ms. `Link.setBaud(rate, fallback)` switches to the requested baud and to the probe's answered baud only when the platform refuses it (WebSerial `open`, serialport `update`); `confirmRaw` reads past broken leftovers until its deadline; `Link.inflightFor(limits)`. `connect` / `openSerial` / `openWebSerial` / `openTcp` take `portSpeed: true | number[]`, `flows`, `verify`, `record`. The page's "Try the rates" keeps working (the minimal form).

- (EN) `raiseSpeed` (oep-core §3.5 at oep-spec d46cc0d, as oep-client-python 1e2b8e4, used on an M5Stack ATOM): after a baud change the link waits `SWITCH_SETTLE_MS` (20 ms) before the first byte, and the new rate is found with a confirm (up to 3, 200 ms each) before anything is measured; a rate whose confirm never comes back says "no confirm at the new rate". The verify runs pipelined at the probe's max_inflight first and, when frames break there (a line that loses bytes while both ways are busy: the ATOM's FTDI at 500 kbaud and up), reads past the leftovers with a confirm and verifies once more one request at a time; the count that passed is `SpeedTrial.inflight` and stays on the link as `Link.inflightCap`, which `Link.exchange` (the host's pipeline) honours until the link is back at the boot speed (end, revert, `backToBase`). `speedText` shows "committed (in flight N)".
